Strength zone

Our strength area is the heart of the gym: barbells, benches and plate-loaded machines laid out so you can move between exercises without waiting. It's set up for compound lifts like squats, presses and rows, with enough space to train safely.

A complete dumbbell range alongside flat, incline and adjustable benches lets beginners learn the basics and experienced lifters push heavy accessory work.
