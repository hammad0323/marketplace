FAQ

Questions? Answered.

What are the gym timings?

Monday to Saturday. Gents: 6:00 AM – 12:00 PM and 4:00 PM – 12:00 AM. Ladies: 12:00 PM – 4:00 PM. The gym is closed on Sunday.

Is there a separate ladies' timing?

Yes. From 12:00 PM to 4:00 PM, Monday to Saturday, the entire gym is reserved for women, with a female-friendly environment and guidance available.

What are the gym fees in DHA Islamabad?

Our monthly plans start at Rs 8,000 (Basic). Premium is Rs 15,000 and Personal Coaching with a dedicated trainer is Rs 35,000 per month. See the membership page for what's included.

Is there a joining fee or a free trial?

There is no joining fee, and you can book a free trial session to see the gym and meet the trainers first — just message us on WhatsApp.

Where exactly is the gym?

We're at Plaza 6, Street 1, Sector C, DHA Phase 5, Islamabad — the basement of the plaza, under the lit-up Muscle Dynamics sign. Tap Get Directions to open Google Maps.

Is parking available?

Parking is available around the plaza in Sector C. If it's your first visit, message us on WhatsApp and we'll guide you in.

Is the gym air-conditioned?

Yes, the whole training floor is air-conditioned, so you can train comfortably even in peak summer.

Do you offer personal training and diet plans?

Yes. Our Premium plan includes a personalised workout plan and diet guidance, and Personal Coaching gives you a dedicated 1-on-1 trainer. Read more on the personal training page.

Do you sell supplements?

Yes — genuine Nutrifactor products are available at the gym. Our staff can help you choose what fits your goal.

Come see the gym, meet the coaches and get a feel for the floor. No joining fee, no pressure.
