Our Story

From a small converting unit to a complete packaging house

Zainco Packaging Industries started with a simple mission: give Pakistani businesses reliable, well-printed packaging at a fair price. Operating from Karachi's SITE industrial area, we have grown step by step — adding woven sack production, BOPP lamination, corrugation and carton converting — so our clients can source all their packaging from a single trusted partner.

Today we serve rice exporters, flour mills, cement and chemical manufacturers, pharmaceutical companies, FMCG brands and fast-growing e-commerce stores with more than 35 packaging products.
